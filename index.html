Company

Marine technology from Vigo, since 2004.

Deliver value through technological development and innovation projects for fishing, aquaculture and the environment.

Marexi was founded in Vigo by technologist Joaquín Gracia Salvador and scientist Iñaki Miniño. Today the team spans engineers, physicists, mathematicians and biologists.

The company operates from technical offices and R&D laboratories in Vigo and Palma de Mallorca, designing, manufacturing and commercialising its own products, with over 20 years of experience in the fishing, aquaculture and food processing industries.

Joaquín Gracia Salvador Co-founder Extensive track record developing technology solutions in microwaves, computer vision and underwater acoustics. Computer engineer, digital electronics and robotics technician and professional diver.
