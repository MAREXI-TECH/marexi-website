How it works

Three stages. Zero human intervention.

Stage 01

Feeding & alignment

Tuna of mixed species, sizes and condition is discharged into a hopper. Transport elements separate and align each fish for individual scanning, regardless of the presence of ice or frost.

Stage 02

3D scanning with AI

The system scans each fish individually using 3D laser profilometry, converting it into a point cloud. AI algorithms and neural networks process the data to determine species, size and weight with industrial-grade precision.

Stage 03

Automatic classification

The operator defines the classification criteria and the system autonomously assigns each fish to its container. Results integrate in real time with the plant's ERP for full traceability and production control.
