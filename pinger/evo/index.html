Technology and performance

A PINGER® for every situation.

PINGER® EVO lets you sail with peace of mind, protecting you from interactions with orcas in a safe and sustainable way. To do so, it features pre-configured acoustic profiles specific to orcas that automatically adapt frequency, pulse duration and stimulus variability to each operating condition. Why different profiles?

PINGER® EVO is an acoustic device developed for different applications where interaction with marine mammal species may occur. For use on recreational vessels, it offers different operating modes developed specifically for orcas.

Easy to use on board

Designed to accompany you while sailing.

To reduce unwanted interactions with orcas, the PINGER® EVO can be towed directly from the stern on a suitable line made fast to a cleat or towing point. Once submerged it switches on by itself and runs the selected profile; when taken out of the water it switches off automatically.

1

Set up

Place the PINGER® EVO in its dock and connect via Bluetooth. Select the desired operating mode in the App.

2

Submerge

Secure it at the stern with a line and lower it into the water. It switches on and starts working by itself — no switches, no extra steps.

3

Sail

The device will run the selected profile while submerged.

Pinger_EVO App

Everything under control from your phone.

The App is the command centre of the PINGER® EVO: it is where you set the operating mode, choosing the most suitable one for each situation.

It also shows battery level, estimated runtime, cumulative operating hours, serial number and active mode, and lets you manage several devices from a single app.

Runs locally over Bluetooth 5.0: no mobile coverage, no Internet, no cloud services and no user account needed.
