Service overview

Underwater acoustic testing in a controlled environment

Our facilities are specifically engineered to conduct controlled, highly repeatable trials under reproducible conditions, with strict adherence to measurement reliability, quality assurance and rigorous data traceability. The acoustic tank is fully conditioned to perform underwater measurements with excellent repeatability and standard-compliant traceability.

Fully controlled environment

The tank is acoustically lined with closed-cell elastomeric foam to minimise reflections and ensure measurement repeatability across every test campaign.

Reference instrumentation

HBK measurement chain: Type 8103 Miniature Hydrophone (sensitivity > −200 dB, 0.1 Hz – 180 kHz) and LAN-XI Type 3161 data acquisition module with dedicated analysis software.

Core capabilities

What we can test

Hydrophone calibration
Sensitivity calibration and frequency-response verification for reference and field hydrophones.

Full acoustic profiling of piezoceramic and electromagnetic transducers: directivity, frequency response and power parameters.

Technical infrastructure

Facility specifications

Tank dimensions
4 m × 2 m × 2 m
Capacity
Approx. 16 m³ (~16,000 litres of water)
Structure
Structural steel S275-JR, reinforced perimetrically with structural tubing and a folded upper profile for high geometric rigidity
Acoustic lining
Internal cladding with a 2 cm closed-cell elastomeric foam attenuation layer to optimise the boundary environment
Reference hydrophone
HBK Type 8103 Miniature Hydrophone — sensitivity > −200 dB, range 0.1 Hz – 180 kHz
Data acquisition
HBK LAN-XI Type 3161 module with dedicated analysis software
Transducer compatibility
Piezoceramic and electromagnetic elements
Logistics
5-tonne integrated overhead gantry crane for precise deployment and immersion of heavy components
