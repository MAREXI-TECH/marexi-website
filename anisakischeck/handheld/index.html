Technical features

Features

  • High-power UV LED (365 nm): uniform, stable light, free of ozone generation
  • UV power: 80 W · emission area: 40 × 40 mm
  • Lithium battery: 16.8 V, 3,400 mAh — up to 1.5 hours of continuous operation
  • Smart temperature control with automatic shut-off and independent cooling fans
  • Tilt safety switch: automatic power cut at potentially dangerous angles for the eyes
  • Instant full power, no warm-up time
  • Service life over 20,000 hours
  • UV body: 140 × 50 × 190 mm · case: 295 × 230 × 95 mm · net weight: 1.0 kg
  • In the box: main body with UV matrix, rechargeable lithium battery, smart charger (110–220 V), certified UV safety goggles
