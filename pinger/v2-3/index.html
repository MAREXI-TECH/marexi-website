How it works

Built to work at sea

Automatic on/off

The automatic on/off switch activated by immersion in water saves battery and increases the device’s operating time.

Low-battery indicator

When the internal circuit detects less than 3% of effective life, a flashing red light comes on: the Pinger has reached the end of its service life and must be replaced with a new one.

Silent mode if lost

To avoid acoustic pollution of the marine environment in case of accidental loss, after 48 hours of uninterrupted immersion the Pinger switches to silent standby mode. It reactivates automatically once taken out of the water.

Fixing and housing

The stainless-steel cable allows any fixing configuration: marine-grade plastic cable ties, shackles, ropes, etc. The housing is an advanced technical polymer, resistant to abrasion and impact, with an additive against degradation from ultraviolet radiation and other agents in the marine environment.

Installation on fishing gear

One Pinger every 200 metres

Pinger® devices have an omnidirectional emission pattern, so their position on the net does not affect acoustic coverage. They are attached to the net with small lines or plastic cable ties through their two steel-cable loops, in the areas where marine mammals are most likely to enter, to prevent them from becoming entangled.

Trawl

Installed horizontally at the mouth of the net.

Gillnet

Installed horizontally, less than 1 metre below the float line.

Purse seine

Installed vertically, more than 10 metres below the float line.

Regulation. This model is developed in accordance with Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/967 of 3 July 2020, which for these Pinger characteristics sets a maximum spacing of 200 metres between devices, with one device fixed at each end of the net or combination of joined nets.
